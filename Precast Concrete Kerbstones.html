Frequently Asked Questions

What are kerbstones used for in road construction?

Kerbstones serve to delineate road edges, channel rainwater drainage into gutters, reinforce road pavements, and prevent vehicles from mounting footpaths.

What is the GST rate applied to concrete kerbstones?

Precast concrete kerbstones carry a standard GST rate of 18%.
