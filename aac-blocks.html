Frequently Asked Questions

What are the main advantages of AAC blocks over red clay bricks?

AAC blocks are lighter (reducing structural load), faster to lay due to larger size and precise dimensions, fire-resistant, and provide superior thermal and acoustic insulation compared to traditional red bricks.

What joint mortar should be used for laying AAC blocks?

It is recommended to use specialized thin-bed polymer mortar (AAC Block Jointing Mortar) with a 2–3mm joint thickness rather than conventional thick cement-sand mortar.

What GST rate applies to AAC blocks?

Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) blocks carry a standard GST rate of 12% in India.
