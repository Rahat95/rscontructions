Frequently Asked Questions

When should I choose 6-inch bricks over 4-inch bricks?

6-inch bricks are recommended for external load-bearing walls, structural masonry, and perimeter boundary walls requiring superior stability, soundproofing, and thermal insulation compared to thinner 4-inch internal partition bricks.

How are 6-inch bricks packaged and delivered?

Bricks are delivered directly to the construction site in organized flatbed tipper loads or stacked neatly on pallets for rapid unloading.

What GST rate applies to 6-inch masonry bricks?

Building masonry bricks carry a standard GST rate of 5% in India.
