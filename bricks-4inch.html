Frequently Asked Questions

Can 4-inch bricks be used for main structural walls?

No. 4-inch brickwork is intended for non-load-bearing internal partition walls. Main exterior structural walls typically require 9-inch brickwork or structural blocks.

How many 4-inch bricks are required per square foot of wall?

Approximately 4.5 to 5 pieces of 4-inch bricks are required per square foot of half-brick thick wall (including mortar joints).

What GST rate applies to 4-inch clay bricks?

Building clay bricks carry a standard GST rate of 5% in India.
