Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Brown River Sand and Black River Sand?

Both are natural river sands, but brown river sand usually features finer grain distribution (Zone III/IV), making it preferred for wall plastering and mortar work, while black sand is often coarser (Zone II) and ideal for heavy RCC casting.

How is Brown River Sand measured for truck delivery?

Brown river sand is delivered and calculated in Brass (1 Brass = 100 Cubic Feet) or by total weight via weighbridge scales.

What GST rate applies to natural brown river sand?

Natural building sand carries a standard GST rate of 5% in India.
