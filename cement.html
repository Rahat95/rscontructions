Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between OPC 53 Grade and PPC Cement?

OPC 53 Grade develops strength rapidly and is preferred for heavy structural RCC casting. PPC releases heat slowly, offers higher resistance to chemical attacks, and reduces thermal cracking, making it ideal for general construction and plastering.

What is the shelf life of a cement bag?

Cement is best used within 90 days of its manufacture date. At RS CONSTRUCTIONS, we ensure fresh stock delivery direct from plants.
