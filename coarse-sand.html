Frequently Asked Questions

Why is coarse sand preferred over fine sand for concrete work?

Coarse sand has larger angular particles with higher particle surface strength and lower surface area, which requires less water-cement ratio while preventing drying cracks in concrete structural members.

What is the difference between Zone I and Zone II sand?

Zone I sand contains a higher proportion of larger coarse particles ($> 2.36\text{ mm}$) suitable for heavy RCC foundations, while Zone II sand has a balanced distribution ideal for general slab and beam concreting.

What GST rate applies to building sand?

Natural and crushed construction coarse sand carries a standard GST rate of 5% in India.
