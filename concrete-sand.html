Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Concrete Sand and Plastering Sand?

Concrete Sand contains larger, coarser, and more angular grains (Zone II) designed to give structural strength to concrete mixes. Plastering Sand uses much finer grains (Zone IV) to ensure a smooth, level wall finish.

How is Concrete Sand supplied for bulk orders?

Concrete sand is delivered via tipper trucks measured in Brass (1 Brass = 100 Cubic Feet) or weighed directly at certified weighbridges.

What GST rate applies to Concrete Sand?

Concrete sand carries a standard GST rate of 5% in India.
