Frequently Asked Questions

Is Crushed Sand (M-Sand) better than River Sand for concrete?

Yes. M-Sand offers uniform gradation, zero silt/clay impurities, and angular particles that create a stronger mechanical bond, resulting in higher overall compressive concrete strength compared to river sand.

How is Crushed Sand delivered and measured?

Crushed Sand is delivered in tipper trucks and measured in standard Brass units (1 Brass = 100 Cubic Feet) or weighed on certified weighbridges.

What GST rate applies to Crushed Sand?

Manufactured crushed sand carries a standard GST rate of 5% in India.
