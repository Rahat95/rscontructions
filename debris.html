Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Murrum soil preferred for plinth filling?

Murrum possesses high load-bearing capacity and minimal swelling-shrinkage characteristics compared to organic topsoil or black cotton soil, making it ideal for stable plinth compaction.

How is soil filling measured for bulk orders?

Soil and murrum filling material are supplied and measured in Brass (1 Brass = 100 Cubic Feet) or by dump truck volume.

What GST rate applies to filling soil and earth material?

Natural filling earth and murrum soil carry a standard GST rate of 5% in India.
