Frequently Asked Questions

What are the benefits of using Washed Sand over unwashed sand?

Washed sand is hydro-processed to remove clay and silt that interfere with cement hydration. Using washed sand increases concrete durability, prevents efflorescence (salt staining), and reduces plaster cracking.

How is Washed Sand measured for delivery?

Washed sand is supplied in standard Brass units (1 Brass = 100 Cubic Feet) or weighed on certified weighbridge scales for truckload deliveries.

What GST rate applies to washed construction sand?

Washed construction sand carries a standard GST rate of 5% in India.
