Frequently Asked Questions

Why must railway ballast be highly angular rather than rounded?

Angular edges interlock with one another, preventing sleepers from shifting sideways or horizontally under the massive weight and vibration of moving trains.

How is railway ballast measured for bulk orders?

Railway ballast is supplied in bulk tipper trucks measured in Brass (1 Brass = 100 Cubic Feet) or weight-checked via certified weighbridges.

What GST rate applies to railway track ballast?

Crushed stone railway ballast carries a standard GST rate of 5% in India.
