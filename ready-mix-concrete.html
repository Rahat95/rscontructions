Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity for Ready Mix Concrete?

Standard delivery starts at a minimum batch size of 1 Transit Mixer capacity (typically 6 cubic meters), though partial loads can be arranged upon request.

How quickly must Ready Mix Concrete be poured after delivery?

Concrete should ideally be poured and compacted within 90 to 120 minutes of batching to ensure optimum strength and workability.

What GST rate applies to Ready Mix Concrete?

Ready Mix Concrete (RMC) is taxed at the standard rate of 18% GST in India.
