Frequently Asked Questions

What is Random Rubble Masonry (RRM)?

Random Rubble Masonry uses rough, un-dressed or roughly hammer-dressed stones of varying sizes bound with cement mortar to form exceptionally strong structural walls and foundations.

How is Rubble Stone measured for bulk orders?

In construction logistics, rubble stone is typically sold in Brass (1 Brass = 100 Cubic Feet) or by weight in Metric Tonnes using weighbridge trucks.

What GST rate applies to natural building stones?

Raw quarry rubble stones carry a standard GST rate of 5% in India.
