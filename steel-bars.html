Frequently Asked Questions

What does 'D' stand for in Fe-550D grade TMT steel?

The 'D' stands for Ductility. Fe-550D bars offer higher percentage elongation compared to standard Fe-550 bars, absorbing shock loads better during seismic activity or thermal stress.

What is the standard length of a TMT steel bar?

Standard TMT steel rebar bundles are supplied in continuous bent or straight lengths of 12 meters (approximately 40 feet) per bar.

What GST rate applies to construction TMT steel bars?

Iron and steel reinforcement bars carry a standard GST rate of 18% in India.
